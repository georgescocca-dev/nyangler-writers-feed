# Bunker Balls Tighten Off the Hook: Bass Setup Building Toward the New Moon
*The bait's stacking from Flynn's Knoll to the Rip and the stripers are starting to lean on it — this is the setup we've been waiting on.*

**PERTH AMBOY, NJ — September 7** — _by Marisol Duarte, Zone Writer_

If you've only got a couple hours this week, spend them working the edges of the bunker schools between Flynn's Knoll and the Rip at the Hook, not out on the flats chasing fluke scraps. That's the call. The bait has been tightening up hard the last several days under the full moon that just passed on the 6th and 7th, and the bass are starting to lean into those schools instead of just picking at the stragglers on the fringe. That's a different behavior than what we saw two weeks ago, and it's the first real sign that the fall pattern is loading up.

We flagged this exact setup last week — said Raritan Bay and the Hook would be the first place the real fall bass bite breaks open, likely within a week to ten days as the nights cooled off. Here we are. The moon work is doing what it's supposed to do. Bunker pods that were loose and scattered across the bay in August are balling up tight now off Keyport and pushing out past Flynn's Knoll on the stronger stages of the tide, and there's noticeably more predator interest underneath them than there was even ten days ago.
