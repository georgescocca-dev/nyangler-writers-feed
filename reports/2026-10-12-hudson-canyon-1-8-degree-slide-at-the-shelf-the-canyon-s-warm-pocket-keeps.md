If the yellowfin bite keeps fading as the water cools, bluefin are likely to be the fish holding the fort longest, since they're built for exactly this kind of transition.

Anyone planning a run should be checking the forecast closely — a 3-to-4-foot wind chop on top of a thinning warm pocket is a rough combination for finding consistent marks.
